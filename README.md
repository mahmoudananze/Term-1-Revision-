# English Revision Hub – Term 1 (2026–2027)

Interactive online revision for students at the **Scientific Institute for Secondary Education**, English Department.
Created by **Dr. Mahmoud Ananzeh**.

🔗 **Open the hub:** https://mahmoudananze.github.io/Term-1-Revision-/

---

## 📚 What's inside

| Page | Grade | Level | What students practise |
|------|-------|-------|------------------------|
| [`index.html`](index.html) | All | – | Home page – students choose their grade |
| [`term1-revision.html`](term1-revision.html) | Grade 12 Advanced | Stage 9 · B2.2 (reading up to C1.1) | Vocabulary, 7 grammar points, Reading 3A & 3B, comparative essay writing, 100-mark mock exam |
| [`grade10.html`](grade10.html) | Grade 10 GLG | Stage 6 · CEFR B1.2 | Vocabulary, 7 grammar lessons, Reading 3A & 3B, Seasons / Shared Living writing, 100-mark mock exam |

### Each grade page includes
- 🃏 **Flashcards** for every word on the list, with a button to hear the word
- 🔗 **Matching, gap-fill, collocations, word forms and spelling** activities
- 🧠 **Grammar notes** with practice sets and typed exercises
- 📖 **Reading practice** with the text beside the questions
- ✍️ **Writing boxes** with a live word count, self-check lists and model answers
- 📝 **A timed mock exam (100 marks):** the 60 reading and language marks are scored automatically
- ✅ **A final checklist** and a progress tracker
- ⭐ **Bonus activities** that aren't in the printed booklets

Every question gives **instant feedback with an explanation**.

---

## 👩‍🎓 For students
1. Open the hub link above on a phone, tablet or computer.
2. Choose your grade.
3. Type your name, then work through the tabs: Vocabulary → Grammar → Reading → Writing.
4. Repeat any activity below 80% (the score turns green at 80%).
5. Finish with the **Mock exam** and the **Checklist**.

> Your scores and writing are saved **only on your own device**. To share your work, take a screenshot of your mock result or use **📋 Copy my answer** and send it to your teacher.

---

## 🛠 For teachers: publishing the site
1. Upload `index.html`, `term1-revision.html` and `grade10.html` to the main folder of this repository.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, branch **main**, folder **/ (root)**, then click **Save**.
4. After 1–2 minutes the site is live at the link above.

Each page is a single self-contained HTML file, with no installation or internet libraries needed.
