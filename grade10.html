Reading & Viewing (60 marks) is taken online. Writing (40 marks) is on paper. GLG means Grade Level Goal. Parts 1, 2, 3A and 4 together are worth 84 marks.

7 · Test-day tips

  • Do the sections in this order: Part 1, Part 2, Part 3A, Part 4, then Part 3B.
  • Never leave a multiple-choice question empty. Guess if needed.
  • For reading, find the answer in the text. Do not use only your own opinion.
  • For writing, plan for 3 minutes: opinion, three ideas, example, conclusion.
  • Leave 3 minutes at the end to check your writing.
  • The exam specification does not state the exam time. Ask your teacher for the timetable.

2 · Part by part

Part 1 · Vocabulary (10)

One text of about 160 words with 5 gaps; choose from 3 options. Themes: Meeting New People, Learning New Things and Come Rain or Shine.

Part 2 · Grammar (10)

One narrative of about 160 words (Writing a Description of Wild Weather) with 5 gaps: past continuous, relative clauses, verb patterns, indirect questions, ability, linkers, comparatives.

Part 3A · Reading (24)

One expository text of 250–270 words on Sharing Things. 8 questions × 3 marks: 4 specific information, 2 details, 1 inference, 1 overall meaning.

Part 3B · Reading (16)

A harder B2.1 text of 250–270 words on Feeling Under the Weather?. 8 × 2 marks: 4 details, 1 inference, 1 connections, 1 prediction, 1 overall meaning.

Part 4 · Writing (40)

Choose Talking About Seasons or Ready for a Shared-Living Experience?. Task 1.1: summary in 3–4 sentences (15). Task 1.2: argumentative text, at least 150 words, all three prompts (25).
